About VIP Billiards

Since 2007, VIP Billiards has been Bayport's premier destination for pool enthusiasts, casual players, and league competitors alike.

Featuring 17 professional-grade pool tables in immaculate condition, a full-service bar, and a welcoming atmosphere, we've built a community of players who appreciate the game and each other.

Whether you're looking to join a competitive league, practice your game, or just enjoy a night out with friends, VIP Billiards offers the perfect environment.

📍 Finding VIP Billiards

From the East: Take Sunrise Hwy exit 51 south. Turn right onto Montauk Highway. We're on the left side after about 1 mile.

From the West: Take Sunrise Hwy exit 50 to Broadway. Turn right onto Broadway, take to the end, make left onto Montauk Highway. VIP is about 1 mile east on the right side of the road.

Landmark: People's Republic Bank is directly across the street.
